FAQ

Basics

What is Proofmarks?

A writing linter for your website. It marks what reads like AI slop on your real page, the way an editor marks a proof, and says why. Your agent can draft a fix, and nothing changes until you accept it. Colin Thornton built it as a tool to visually review one of the essays on Torta's website. The original inspiration comes from a proof of concept Jhey Tompkins built and shared on X.

Why on the real page, and not in an editor?

Writing reads differently in its layout, next to its headings, images and the rest of the page. Reviewing it there catches what an editor window hides. And Accept writes the change into your source file, so there's nothing to copy back.

Why not just ask a model whether my writing sounds like AI?

Asked broadly, a model flags too much and changes its mind with the wording. When we tried it on one of our essays, broad questions flagged about 170 of 231 sentences, and the same sentence scored 0.81 or 0.28 depending on how we asked. Proofmarks narrows the question instead. Code finds the candidates for each rule. A decision model answers one yes-or-no question about each, held to a line calibrated on machine habits and human writing. The same question checks every rewrite before you see it.

Is it an AI detector?

No. It marks habits, and people write these habits too. A mark says the sentence would read better without it, and nothing about who wrote it. It isn't built to help writing slip past a detector. The point is writing people want to read.

How accurate is it?

On our labeled set it catches 110 of 119 planted habits, and marks 9 places in 19 passages of public-domain human writing, such as Darwin, Melville and Kennedy. On your site, each rule's line then moves with what you accept and dismiss.

Does it rewrite my writing for me?

Only when you ask, and never without you. Your agent drafts each rewrite, the decision model checks it, and you accept it, dismiss it or ask for another. Proposals change nothing, and Undo reverses any decision.

Does it learn my style?

Yes. After enough of your decisions on a rule, its line moves toward what you keep and dismiss. That happens on your machine, from the decision logs beside your reviews.

Is it free?

Yes. We've set a healthy daily limit on the shared decision model to start, and we'll adjust it as people use it and tell us how it goes. If the limit is reached, reviews pause until midnight UTC, or you can set your own Jev key.

Setup

Does it work with any framework?

Yes. proofmarks dev runs in front of your dev server, so the framework doesn't matter. Setup recognizes Next.js, Astro, Eleventy, Vite, Jekyll, Hugo, Jinja templates and plain HTML, and finds your dev server's address.

Where does my writing need to be?

In local files: Markdown, MDX, HTML or Jinja-style templates. Template code is protected, and edits never cross it. Content in a CMS isn't supported yet.

Which agents can I use?

Any agent that connects to MCP servers. Setup connects Claude Code, Codex and pi for you.

Do I need a key or an account?

No. Proofmarks uses Jev, a decision model from TypeSafe, through Torta's relay, which pays for it. To skip the relay for judging, set your own Jev key. To use a different decision model, fork Proofmarks and implement its judge contract. Each model needs its own calibration, since every rule's line is tuned to the model.

Do I need Node?

No. The standalone program has it built in. With Node 22 or later, the npm package works too.

Can I use it in production?

You can, with some setup, but we don't recommend it. Proofmarks is built for development, where it edits the source files on your machine. If you add it to a production site anyway, put it behind a feature flag that only you can turn on.

Can my team share it?

Yes. Reviews and their decision logs are files in your project. Commit them, and everyone sees the same marks and shares each rule's learned line.

Can I turn rules off, or add my own?

You can turn any rule on or off for a project, in proofmarks.config.json. Title case headings start off. Your own rules aren't supported natively, but you're welcome to fork Proofmarks and add them. Configuration

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The marks fit a phone's screen. Tap one, and its proof opens at the bottom of the screen. To see it, open proofmarks.dev on your phone and select Try it.

Privacy

What leaves my machine?

The passages being judged. The decision model has to read them, so Torta's relay passes them on without saving them. Rewrites go to the agent you choose, and a feedback report goes to us only when you okay it. Your site and your files stay local.

Does it track me?

Not unless you turn on usage counts. If you do, it sends counts of the marks you keep or dismiss, and one for your install, never your writing. Does proofmarks.dev track me?

It counts visits and clicks without cookies or your location, and not at all if your browser asks not to be tracked. That's separate from Proofmarks itself.

Help

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Two ways. Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Or tell your agent what happened. With Proofmarks connected, it writes up a report for you to check, and sends it to us when you say so.
