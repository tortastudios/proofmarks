# I got rejected from 47 jobs last year.

Here's what I learned. 👇

For six months, I applied everywhere. Startups. Agencies. Big tech. I tailored every resume, rewrote every cover letter, and prepared for every interview like it was the only one.

47 rejections.

At first, it crushed me. But somewhere around rejection number 30, something shifted.

I realized rejection isn't a verdict. It's data.

Every "no" taught me something. One interviewer told me my portfolio was too long. Another said I talked about my team more than myself. A third said, kindly, that I seemed nervous. All of them were right.

So I cut my portfolio in half. I learned to say "I" when I meant me. I practiced interviews with a friend until they felt boring.

Application number 48? I got the offer. And honestly, it's a better job than any of the 47 I didn't get.

The lesson: your setbacks are not the end of your story. They're the foundation of your comeback.

If you're in the middle of a job search right now, keep going. You're closer than you think. 💪
