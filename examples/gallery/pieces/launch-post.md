# Introducing Tidewater Sync

Today, we're thrilled to announce Tidewater Sync, a groundbreaking new way for teams to keep their files, notes, and projects perfectly in sync across every device.

## Why We Built It

Modern teams are more distributed than ever. Designers work on tablets, engineers work on laptops, and managers approve everything from their phones between meetings. Yet the tools that keep work in sync have barely changed in a decade. Files get duplicated, versions conflict, and somewhere along the way the latest draft becomes "final_v3_REAL.docx."

We knew there had to be a better way.

## What It Does

Tidewater Sync isn't just another file sharing tool. It's a seamless, intelligent layer that sits beneath the apps you already use and keeps everything consistent, everywhere, all the time.

- **Instant sync.** Changes appear on every device in under a second.
- **Conflict-free editing.** When two people edit the same file, Tidewater merges their changes automatically instead of making copies.
- **Offline by default.** Work on a plane, in a tunnel, or in a cabin without signal. Everything syncs when you reconnect.

Behind the scenes, Tidewater uses a new synchronization engine our team spent two years building. It was designed from the ground up to handle everything from a single text file to a forty-gigabyte video project.

## Built for Teams of Every Size

Whether you're a solo freelancer or a global enterprise, Tidewater Sync scales effortlessly to meet your needs. Our beta testers, who ranged from a three-person architecture studio to a newsroom with four hundred reporters, saw an average of five hours saved per person each week.

"Tidewater has completely transformed how our team works," says Dana Whitfield, operations lead at Corvid Media. "I can't imagine going back."

## Pricing and Availability

Tidewater Sync is available today for macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. A free plan includes up to five gigabytes of storage. Team plans start at eight dollars per person per month, and every plan comes with a thirty-day free trial.
