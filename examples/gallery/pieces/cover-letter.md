# Cover letter: Product Designer, Harrow Labs

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the Product Designer position at Harrow Labs. As a passionate and detail-oriented designer with over five years of experience crafting intuitive digital experiences, I am confident that my skills and background make me an ideal fit for your team.

In my current role at Pellucid Health, I lead design for our patient portal, which serves more than two hundred thousand people each month. Last year I redesigned the appointment booking flow after noticing that nearly a third of patients abandoned it on the insurance step. By moving insurance to the end and letting people save a draft, we cut abandonment from 31% to 12%. I did the research, the wireframes, and the final screens, and I worked closely with two engineers to ship it in six weeks.

What excites me most about Harrow Labs is your commitment to making complex financial tools accessible to everyone. I have long believed that good design is not just about aesthetics, it is about empowering people to take control of their lives. Your recent work on the budgeting dashboard is a perfect example of this philosophy in action, and I would love the opportunity to contribute to that mission.

Beyond my technical skills, I bring a collaborative spirit, a growth mindset, and a deep commitment to user-centered design. I thrive in fast-paced environments and enjoy working with cross-functional teams to turn ambiguous problems into elegant solutions.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience could help Harrow Labs continue to innovate and grow. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
Jonah Ferreira
